X (Twitter) Video Downloader

Paste the link to an X post and save the video in HD. Free, no signup, no watermark.

How to download a video from X (Twitter)

  1. Open the post with the video on X and copy its link (tap Share → Copy link).
  2. Paste the link into the box above and click Download.
  3. Video Hunter finds the video and lists every available quality.
  4. Click the quality you want and the video is saved to your device.

Both x.com and twitter.com links work, including links to posts inside a thread or a quote tweet.

Frequently asked questions about X videos

Can I download X videos for free?
Yes. Video Hunter is free, needs no account, and adds no watermark.

Do I need to log in to X?
No. You only need the link to the post. Video Hunter fetches the video for you.

Which quality can I get?
Whatever X provides for that video, usually from 480p up to 1080p or the original resolution.

Why can't I download a specific video?
The post may have been deleted, the account may be private, or the video may be restricted in your region. Images and GIFs are not supported, only videos.

How do I save an X video on an iPhone?
Tap and hold the download button and choose Download Linked File, or use a file manager such as Documents by Readdle. More tips are in the FAQ.
