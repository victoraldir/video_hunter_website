Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading videos with Video Hunter. Still stuck? Paste a link on the home page or message @MyVideoHunterBot on Telegram.

How do I download a video from X (Twitter), Reddit or Bluesky?
Copy the link to the post that contains the video, paste it into the box on the home page and click Download. Video Hunter finds the video and shows a download button for each quality the platform offers.

Is Video Hunter free?
Yes. Video Hunter is free and requires no account or signup. Videos are streamed directly from the platforms' content delivery networks and are not stored on our servers.

Can I download videos with a Telegram bot instead?
Yes. Send the video link to @MyVideoHunterBot on Telegram and the bot replies with a download link.

I am not able to save files to my iPhone or iPad. What should I do?
iOS Safari often opens the video in a player instead of saving it. Tap and hold the download button and choose Download Linked File, or use a file manager such as Documents by Readdle to save the video to your device.

What should I do if the video plays instead of downloading?
On mobile, tap and hold the video until the download options appear. On desktop, right-click the video and select Save link as.

Where are the files I downloaded stored?
Downloaded videos are stored in your device's default download folder.

Why can't I download a specific video?
The post may have been deleted, the account may be private, or the video may be restricted in your region. Only videos are supported: images and GIFs cannot be downloaded.

Is there a daily download limit?
There is no download limit on the website. The Telegram bot and the X reply bots may throttle responses during busy periods.

Does Video Hunter store the videos I download?
No. Video Hunter does not store videos on its servers. It is a proxy that streams the video straight from the platform's content delivery network. See the privacy policy for details.

Which video qualities can I download?
Whatever the platform provides, usually from 480p up to 1080p or the original resolution. Video Hunter lists one download button per available quality.

Please make sure you have the right to download the content you save. Video Hunter respects content creators.
