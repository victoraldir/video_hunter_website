Video Hunter Telegram Bot

Send a video link in Telegram and get a download link back. No app to install, no signup.

Open @MyVideoHunterBot

How to download videos with the bot

  1. Open @MyVideoHunterBot in Telegram and press Start.
  2. Send the link to a post with a video from X (Twitter), Reddit or Bluesky.
  3. The bot replies with a download link. Open it on any device to save the video.

It works in the Telegram app on iOS, Android, desktop and in the web client.

Frequently asked questions about the bot

Is the bot free?
Yes, and it needs no registration.

Which platforms does it support?
X (Twitter), Reddit and Bluesky — the same platforms as the website.

Why did the bot not reply?
It can be busy or rate limited by the platform. Wait a moment and send the link again, and make sure the post is public and contains a video.

Does the bot store my videos?
No. Videos are streamed directly from the platform's content delivery network and are not stored on our servers.
