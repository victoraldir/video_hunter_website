Bluesky Video Downloader

Paste a bsky.app post link and save the video. Free, no signup, no watermark.

How to download a video from Bluesky

  1. Open the Bluesky post with the video and copy its link (tap Share → Copy link).
  2. Paste the link into the box above and click Download.
  3. Video Hunter fetches the video from Bluesky.
  4. Click download and the video is saved to your device.

Links look like https://bsky.app/profile/<handle>/post/<id>. Posts from accounts on custom domains work too.

Frequently asked questions about Bluesky videos

Is it free?
Yes. Video Hunter is free, needs no account, and adds no watermark.

Do I need to log in to Bluesky?
No. You only need the link to a public post.

Which links are supported?
Any bsky.app/profile/…/post/… link, including posts shared from the Bluesky app.

Why does a Bluesky video fail to download?
The post may have been deleted or the account taken down, or the post may not contain a video at all. Only posts with a video attachment can be downloaded. More answers in the FAQ.
